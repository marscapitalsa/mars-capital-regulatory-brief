A Swiss sanctions-list amendment is already in force. The IAM should complete the control steps below; the three foreign-law developments remain conditional on the stated nexus or use case.

Action required
Switzerland · 11 Aug

Switzerland adds 53 Russia-related sanctions targets

What changed: the Swiss Annex 8 update added nine individuals and 44 entities, amended six existing records and took effect on 11 August at 23:00. Five Annex 2 end-user entries were also amended.

In force since 11 August 2026, 23:00 · internal completion date for the full-book rescreen: to be set internally

Action: load the current SECO list, re-screen relevant clients, UBOs/controllers, holdings, issuers and transaction parties, resolve potential ownership or control matches, and apply the Swiss freezing, non-availability and SECO-reporting rules where a confirmed match exists. The detailed page separates mandatory steps from the proposed internal control response.

Potentially relevant
EU / Lenz & Staehelin · 03 Aug

EU AI Act: current transparency rules and revised implementation dates

What changed: the AI Act's general application date arrived on 2 August, but Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 moved the core high-risk-system requirements to 2 December 2027 or 2 August 2028. It also gives legacy generative-AI providers until 2 December 2026 for machine-readable marking and introduces new prohibited practices from that date.

Applicable dates: 2 August and 2 December 2026 · later high-risk dates depend on classification

Relevant if: the IAM has an EU establishment or uses AI output in the EU, supplies an AI system into the EU, operates an EU-facing chatbot, publishes covered AI-generated content, or uses an Annex III high-risk use case. Ordinary internal drafting or analysis in Switzerland is not automatically an Annex III high-risk use.

Potentially relevant
UK · 06 Aug

UK adds six Russian banks, seven other targets and six vessels

What changed: the UK Sanctions List added 13 Russia-regime designations and six ship specifications. The designated banks include Ozon Bank, Roseximbank, Center-Invest, Realist Bank, Bank Stavr and Teleport Bank.

UK designation date: 6 August 2026 · Swiss internal review date: to be set internally

Relevant if: a client, UBO, payment, portfolio holding, bank, custodian or transaction has a UK nexus, or a service provider contractually applies the UK list. The UK update does not itself amend Swiss sanctions law.

EU · 07 Aug

EU lists five Russian defence-industry executives

What changed: the Council imposed an asset freeze and a prohibition on making funds or economic resources available to five senior figures at Russian defence and military-technology companies.

EU listing date: 7 August 2026 · Swiss internal review date: to be set internally

Relevant if: the IAM, a client, UBO, issuer, payment, bank or custodian has an EU nexus or applies the EU list. The separate Swiss amendment effective 11 August must be screened under the current SECO list and is covered above.
