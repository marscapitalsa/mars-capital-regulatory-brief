FINMA · 10 Nov

AMLA advisory activities: check the service, not its label

What changed: FINMA Guidance 06/2026 explains how the expanded AMLA applies to specified advisory services. Advice that contributes to an in-scope corporate or property transaction can qualify without control over client funds.

Why it matters: High if the IAM helps form, fund or administer entities, supports acquisitions or property transactions, or provides domicile services. The detailed analysis covers scope tests, professional thresholds, exemptions and practical cases.

1 December 2026 notification if covered activity existed on 1 October · later starts: see the two-month rule

Action: Map the services actually provided and the applicable exclusions. An SO-monitored IAM should confirm the notification route with its SO, not assume a new standalone SRO application is required.

FINMA · 10 Nov

AMLO-FINMA expressly requires understanding ownership and control

What changed: The adopted revision adds Article 9b: financial intermediaries must understand the counterparty’s ownership and control structure. FINMA describes this as expressing an existing principle, not a blanket new re-identification exercise.

Why it matters: High for corporate clients, trusts and multi-layered structures. A beneficial-owner name alone may not explain who ultimately controls the client and how.

Effective 1 January 2027 · internal gap-review date: To be set internally

Action: Review whether the available KYC information explains the chain of ownership and actual control; supplement it proportionately where the structure or risk requires.

FINMA · 10 Nov

AMLO-FINMA specifies organisational sanctions controls

What changed: The adopted revision adds Article 30 and applies specified monitoring, internal-instruction, compliance, training and outsourcing requirements by analogy to preventing breaches of Swiss sanctions under the Embargo Act.

Why it matters: High for the Swiss IAM’s sanctions framework, especially where clients, beneficial owners or payment chains have exposure to restricted parties or activities.

Effective 1 January 2027 · existing sanctions duties continue now · internal review: To be set internally

Action: Check the allocation of controls between the IAM, outsourced compliance and transaction providers, including pre-execution checks, escalation and ongoing monitoring.

FINMA · 10 Nov

CDB 27 recognition: coordinate bank onboarding changes

What changed: FINMA recognised the revised Swiss banks’ due-diligence agreement, CDB/VSB 27, as a minimum standard. It starts on 1 January 2027, with a four-month transition and no wholesale replacement of existing forms required.

Why it matters: Medium; higher if the IAM collects bank KYC documents under delegated onboarding arrangements. Bank requirements and the IAM’s own regulatory rules should not be assumed identical.

Effective 1 January 2027 · four-month transition under Art. 68 · internal coordination: To be set internally

Action: Ask relevant banks which forms and digital processes they will accept or require, and align any delegated collection process. Do not reopen every existing file solely because the agreement changes.

UK FCDO · 12 Nov

UK lists Russian energy companies and specifies nine ships

What changed: The UK Russia notice adds 11 designations and nine ship specifications and varies one entry. The companies include SIBUR Holding, Slavneft, Vostok Oil, NIPIGAZ and SUEK.

Why it matters: High if the IAM has a matched issuer, counterparty, ownership or payment exposure and an applicable UK legal or provider-policy nexus; otherwise conditional.

UK measures dated 12 November 2026 · internal exposure-review date: To be set internally

Action: Review exact entities and ownership/control, instruments and transaction providers. Distinguish asset-freeze designations from the specific shipping restrictions; UK measures are not automatically Swiss law.

UK FCDO · 10 Nov

UK updates three existing Russia-regime entries

What changed: The UK varied the existing entries for ALT CAPITAL, Narmina DADASHOVA and Vladislav GROMOV. The notice does not add three new sanctioned persons.

Why it matters: Low unless one of the entries produces a client, beneficial-owner or counterparty match.

Entry updates dated 10 November 2026 · internal review: To be set internally

Action: Use the updated identifiers and statements when resolving a relevant match. Do not describe the variation notice as three new asset-freeze designations.
