Priority 1

Action required

The publication is not a new legal rule. An internal review is required because the IAM uses a business introducer and ASG expressly requires its model to be adapted to each manager's organisation, activities, services and client base.

Action required
ASG · 26 Aug

ASG publishes an editable model agreement for business introducers

What changed: ASG published a model business-introducer agreement in French, German and Italian as a structured starting point for intermediaries who bring prospective wealth-management or investment-advisory clients to an IAM. The model limits the introducer to introductions, leaves client acceptance and onboarding with the IAM, and addresses cross-border conduct, remuneration, independent status, confidentiality, liability and termination.

No external deadline stated · internal review date: to be set internally

Action: compare the existing agreement and the introducer's actual conduct with the ASG model and the accompanying PwC checklist. Resolve any gap between the written mandate and actual activity, and document the cross-border, AML, remuneration, employment-status and data-protection controls that apply to the relationship.

Priority 2

Potentially relevant

This item requires attention only where the IAM or its outsourced compliance provider relies on the UK web search tool for sanctions screening or quality control.

Potentially relevant
UK · 11 Sep

UK explains how its sanctions search can produce incomplete results

What changed: the UK government published detailed instructions for exact, partial and fuzzy searches. Fuzzy search is off by default; exact phrases and early use of filters can create false negatives; and search results do not cover the separate ownership-and-control analysis for unlisted entities.

No external deadline stated · internal review date: to be set internally

Relevant if: manual UK-list searches form part of onboarding, periodic review, transaction screening, escalation work or oversight of an external screening provider. The guidance does not create a new Swiss obligation and UK restrictions require an applicable UK nexus or contractual adoption by a bank, custodian or other counterparty.
