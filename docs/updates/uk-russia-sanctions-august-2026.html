UK sanctions · Foreign-regime update

UK adds Russian banks, supply-chain targets and six vessels

What changed: on 6 August 2026 the UK Sanctions List recorded 13 new designations and six new ship specifications under the Russia regime. The additions include six Russian banks and entities connected with energy, military supply chains and the LNG trade. [S1, update of 6 August] [S2, entries RUS3701–RUS3719]

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Conditional. The UK measures are not Swiss law. They matter where a relevant person, transaction, payment, security, bank, custodian or service provider is subject to UK sanctions or contractually applies the UK list.
Responsible person / function
Proposed internal allocation:
Primary exposure check: outsourced AML/compliance provider
Internal accountable owner: designated AML Officer or the Director holding that function. UK-law nexus and ownership/control questions: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure: in-house Risk Manager and Portfolio Management. Material restriction, escalation or reporting decision: Directors.
Due / trigger
UK designation date: 6 August 2026. The official update states no separate deadline for a Swiss IAM. Internal nexus and exposure-review date: To be set internally. [S1] [S2]
Status
Conditional review
  • Establish whether any affected client, UBO, payment, bank, custodian, security or service provider has a UK nexus or is screened under a UK-list contractual standard.
  • If that condition applies, screen the 13 designated persons and entities and the six specified ships using the current official list, including aliases and identifiers in the underlying entries. [S2]
  • For the six listed banks, check payments to, from or through the bank and any correspondent-banking dependency. The official entries impose the stated correspondent-banking and payment-processing prohibition in addition to asset-freeze measures. [S2, RUS3709–RUS3714]
  • Do not treat the UK update as a Swiss freeze instruction unless a Swiss measure or another applicable legal or contractual basis is identified.
Evidence
No general evidence requirement for a Swiss IAM arises from the UK listings alone. If a UK or contractual nexus is confirmed, retain the nexus conclusion and screening or escalation record required by the applicable procedure.

Targets most likely to affect financial flows

  • Six banks: Ozon Bank, State Specialized Russian Export-Import Bank (Roseximbank), Center-Invest, Realist Bank, Bank Stavr and Teleport Bank. Their entries state asset-freeze measures and prohibitions on correspondent-banking relationships and processing payments. [S2, RUS3709–RUS3714]
  • Other designations: Northern Engineering, Frion Ship Management, Metallkomplekt, Nikom, Promsisz, Technolux and an individual listed as Zhdanov. [S2, RUS3701 and RUS3703–RUS3708]
  • Specified vessels: Arctic Express, Perseas, Torvian, Asteras, Visund and Zenturo. The list gives their IMO identifiers and the applicable shipping and trade restrictions. [S2, RUS3702 and RUS3715–RUS3719]

Swiss-law position

The UK list is a foreign sanctions regime and does not itself amend the Swiss Ukraine-related measures. The SECO regime page remains the relevant official source for Swiss adoption and operative Swiss restrictions. [S3]
