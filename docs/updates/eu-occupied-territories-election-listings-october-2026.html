EU · 12 Oct · Potentially relevant

EU lists 77 people over Russian elections in occupied Ukrainian territories

What changed: The EU designated 74 election candidates and three members of Russia’s Central Election Commission. Listed individuals face asset freezes, non-availability restrictions and EU travel bans. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. Medium conditional relevance for client, beneficial-owner and connected-person screening, particularly where a customer profile includes political or public functions.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Relevant where EU law or an EU-list contractual policy applies. Participation in these elections is the reason for the individual listings; Russian nationality alone is not the trigger.
Responsible person / function
Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material restrictions, exceptions and policy changes: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
EU measures effective 12 October 2026 · internal review: To be set internally.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • Confirm that a provider applying EU lists loaded the 77 additions and use the official annex to resolve identity matches. [S1, S2]
  • Where a match arises, determine whether an EU person, transaction chain or counterparty policy supplies the nexus before deciding the transaction treatment.
  • Assess any new information about political functions in the existing client review; the publication does not create a blanket restriction on Russian clients.
Evidence
Updated screening result, identity review, applicable nexus or counterparty policy and documented decision.
