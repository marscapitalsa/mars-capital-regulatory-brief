Switzerland expands Belarus sanctions

What changed: the Federal Council adopted further measures concerning Belarus, including expanded goods restrictions and new financial and service prohibitions. The financial measures include restrictions involving specified crypto-assets or central-bank digital currencies and transactions with Belarus-established crypto-service providers and exchange or transfer platforms. [S1] [S2]

Legal status. These are binding Swiss measures, not an EU-only development. The restrictions have applied since 19 September. The question for the IAM is therefore whether a client, UBO, mandate, instrument, fund, wallet, custodian, counterparty or payment chain creates exposure to a prohibited person, platform, asset or service.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions Crypto
Applies to Swiss persons and entities within the scope of the ordinance. Portfolio relevance depends on actual Belarus-linked exposure.
Responsible person / function
Primary: AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Exposure identification: Portfolio Management and Operations. Legal interpretation: in-house Legal Counsel. Independent challenge: Risk Manager. Material restrictions or exits: Directors.
Due / trigger
Restrictions in force from 19 September 2026. Internal completion date for the documented exposure review: To be set internally. Any freeze or SECO report required by the ordinance is not postponed by that internal date.
Status
Screening confirmation required
  • Confirm that the screening provider loaded the Swiss changes and record the effective version or update date.
  • Re-screen clients, UBOs, controllers, signatories, wallets, counterparties and payment beneficiaries.
  • Search mandates and look-through information for relevant Belarus-linked issuers, platforms, crypto-service providers, decentralised platforms and service arrangements.
  • Ask custodians, brokers and relevant execution venues to confirm their treatment of affected assets or counterparties where the IAM cannot see the full chain.
  • Escalate confirmed or possible matches under the freezing, non-availability, clarification and SECO-reporting process.
  • If no exposure is identified, retain the search perimeter, date, data sources and conclusion.
Evidence
Vendor-update confirmation; search and re-screening records; portfolio and counterparty queries; custodian or broker responses where obtained; match-resolution files; SECO correspondence where applicable; and documented no-exposure conclusion if the review is negative.

What the review should distinguish

  • Person or entity exposure: listing and ownership or control by a listed person.
  • Asset exposure: a specified crypto-asset or central-bank digital currency subject to the new restriction.
  • Platform exposure: direct or indirect transactions with a covered Belarus-established crypto-service provider or exchange or transfer platform.
  • Service exposure: managed security services and other newly restricted services identified in the official amendment.
  • Indirect exposure: a fund, AMC, intermediary, wallet or payment route can require review even when the client itself is not Belarusian.
