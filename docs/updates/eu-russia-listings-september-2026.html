European Union · Sanctions · 22–24 September 2026

EU renews Russia-related listings and adds a hybrid-threat designation

What changed: the Council renewed the territorial-integrity restrictive measures for 36 months, until 22 September 2029. More than 3,000 persons and entities remain subject to asset freezes and non-availability restrictions; the review did not renew three individuals and one entity and removed three deceased persons. Two days later, the Council listed Xenia Fedorova under the hybrid-threat regime. [S1] [S2]

Swiss-law distinction. These EU measures are not automatically binding on a Swiss IAM merely because they are EU sanctions. They matter where EU law has an applicable nexus or a bank, custodian, broker, fund or other counterparty contractually applies the EU lists.
