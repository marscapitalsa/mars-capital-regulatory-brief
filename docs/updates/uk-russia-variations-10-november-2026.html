Why it matters: Low unless one of the entries produces a client, beneficial-owner or counterparty match.

Variations rather than additions

The notice expressly identifies three variations and shows changed text. Review the entry and the measures actually imposed rather than inferring that the notice creates a new general restriction. [S1]

Practical application

If screening produces a potential match to one of the named entries, compare it with the current official identifiers and particulars. If there is no relevant exposure, this is an informational update rather than a reason to reopen unrelated files. [S1]
