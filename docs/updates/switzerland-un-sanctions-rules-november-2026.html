Federal Council · Sanctions · Effective 1 November 2026

Financial-sanctions rules harmonised across four UN regimes

What changed: the Federal Council amended the Taliban, ISIL/Al-Qaeda, DPRK and Iran sanctions ordinances. The changes align financial provisions on reporting, credits to blocked accounts and annual reporting of frozen-asset balances; the Iran ordinance also expands specified exemptions for international organisations and humanitarian transfers. [S1] [S2]

Practical effect. This is primarily a sanctions-procedure update rather than a reason to assume the IAM has affected exposure. The required action is to ensure the existing freeze and reporting workflow contains the new rules and is capable of producing the annual balance report if the trigger ever occurs.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions AML
The ordinances are Swiss law. Relationship-specific steps apply only if the IAM holds, manages or becomes aware of affected assets or transactions.
Responsible person / function
Primary: AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Account and payment handling: Operations and custodian liaison. Legal interpretation: in-house Legal Counsel. Control design: Risk Manager. Policy approval: Directors.
Due / trigger
Amendments effective 1 November 2026. Where frozen assets have been reported, annual balance at 31 December is reported by 15 February. Other internal preparation dates: To be set internally.
Status
Procedure update required
  • Add immediate SECO notification to the procedure for a person or entity holding, managing or becoming aware of affected assets.
  • Document how interest, earnings and payments due under pre-existing contracts may be credited to blocked accounts while remaining blocked.
  • Document the treatment and immediate reporting of third-party credits to blocked accounts.
  • Create a control that identifies every previously reported frozen-asset relationship and produces the 31 December balance for submission by 15 February.
  • Update the legal reference table for the Taliban, ISIL/Al-Qaeda, DPRK and Iran ordinances and communicate the changes to the outsourced compliance provider.
  • Do not apply the expanded Iran humanitarian or international-organisation exemptions without confirming that their stated conditions are met.
Evidence
Revised sanctions procedure; reporting and annual-balance templates; legal-reference matrix; system or manual control description; communication to the outsourced provider; test or walkthrough record; and Directors' approval where the internal policy requires it.

What is mandatory and what is conditional?

  • Mandatory framework: the procedure must reflect the Swiss rules once they enter into force.
  • Conditional relationship action: freezing, transaction handling and SECO reporting arise only when the factual trigger under the relevant ordinance exists.
  • Conditional annual return: the 15 February balance report is relevant to a financial institution that has reported frozen assets; it is not a general nil return for every IAM.
  • Internal date: the law fixes 1 November and the applicable 15 February deadline but does not prescribe a separate earlier project date; any such date must therefore be set internally.
