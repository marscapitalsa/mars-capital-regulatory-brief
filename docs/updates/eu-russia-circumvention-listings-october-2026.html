EU · 15 Oct · Potentially relevant

EU targets payment agents and third-country sanctions-circumvention networks

What changed: The EU listed three people and nine entities linked to sanctioned-goods imports, oil and financial circumvention. They include Movers Group, DT Inier, United Stream, Turkish trade firms and oil/shipping intermediaries in the UAE, India and Seychelles. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. High conditional relevance where Russian clients use payment agents or third-country intermediaries. The location of an intermediary outside Russia does not remove the need to check its precise listing and role.

Relevant to EU-linked or contractually EU-screened payment chains, clients, beneficial owners, counterparties and portfolio assets. Swiss adoption was not identified by 18 October.

Application to the IAM

  • Screen the exact new entities and individuals using Regulation 2026/2333 and confirm the provider loaded the 15 October delta. [S1, S2]
  • Where relevant, review whether payments or client business involve United Stream, Movers Group, DT Inier, Begler Tekstil, Medse Group or the oil/shipping entities identified in the annex.
  • For a possible match, establish identity, ownership and control, the EU or contractual nexus and the actual payment chain with the executing bank before deciding whether execution is permitted.
Evidence
Delta screening, intermediary and payment-chain identification, nexus analysis, bank response and decision.
