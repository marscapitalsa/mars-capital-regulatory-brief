FINMA · 20 Oct · Potentially relevant

FINMA approves Switzerland’s first spot Bitcoin ETF

What changed: FINMA approved the first Swiss exchange-traded retail fund investing directly in Bitcoin, with a fund-specific diversification exemption. FINMA stressed that the wrapper does not remove Bitcoin volatility or the risk of substantial losses. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to a Swiss IAM. High for a mandate considering crypto-linked instruments; otherwise informational. Approval of one fund is not a suitability assessment for the IAM’s clients or a general diversification exemption for portfolios.

Product approval versus mandate suitability

FINMA describes the ETF as a Swiss alternative investment fund with specific risk. Its diversification exemption applies to the particular fund. FINMA emphasises that Bitcoin remains highly volatile, that clients can incur substantial losses and that position size matters. [S1]

Which conduct test applies

For portfolio management or portfolio-level investment advice, Art. 12 FinSA requires information on financial circumstances, investment objectives, knowledge and experience. For transaction-specific advice without considering the full portfolio, Art. 11 sets the appropriateness test; client classification and statutory exceptions also matter. The announcement emphasises clear warnings and proper suitability assessment where applicable. [S1] [S2]

Custody exposure is not identical

FINMA recalls its January guidance on operational and custody risk. For an ETF allocation, distinguish custody of the fund units from custody of Bitcoin inside the fund. As an application to the IAM, review the fund documentation and the arrangements actually relied on rather than assuming the IAM itself holds the underlying crypto. [S1] [S3]

Execution: Portfolio Management for product and mandate assessment. In-house Risk Manager: product-risk review. Outsourced compliance and in-house Legal Counsel: conduct-rule review. Directors: oversight under the IAM’s product governance.
Due / trigger
Apply existing FinSA obligations when the relevant service is provided. No new firm-wide implementation deadline was announced.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  1. Before first use in a mandate, review product terms and disclosed volatility, loss and custody risks. Check mandate constraints and document why the proposed allocation and position size fit the portfolio, where suitability applies. [S1] [S2]
  2. Where the instrument is offered or recommended, distinguish the client service and applicable assessment, and ensure the risk information makes the downside clear. [S1] [S2]
Evidence
Relevant fund documents, mandate and client assessment, risk disclosure, and the rationale for the proposed allocation.
