FINMA · Consultation · 30 September 2026

Direct transmission: FINMA consults on revised Circular 2017/6

What changed: following Parliament's 19 June amendment of Article 42c FINMASA, FINMA opened consultation on a partial revision of Circular 2017/6. FINMA says the legislative amendment clarifies the distinction between transmissions for financial-market supervisory purposes and transmissions for other purposes and introduces a general presumption, for supervised institutions, concerning confidentiality and purpose limitation for supervisory-purpose transfers. [S1]

Draft only. The consultation closes on 27 November 2026. The proposed circular is not yet final and should not be used as if it already changed the conditions for a specific disclosure.

Conditional action plan

Applicability
Cross-border Data transfers
Relevant where the IAM sends non-public information directly to a foreign financial-market supervisory authority or relies on Article 42c FINMASA for another cross-border disclosure.
Responsible person / function
Primary: in-house Legal Counsel
Transmission facts and data inventory: the requesting business function and outsourced compliance provider. Confidentiality, data-protection and operational controls: Legal and Risk Manager. Material disclosure approval and oversight: Directors.
Due / trigger
If the IAM wishes to comment, consultation response by 27 November 2026. Otherwise, monitor the final circular. Any current disclosure must be analysed under the law and rules applicable at the time.
Status
Consultation / applicability check
  • Identify whether the IAM has made, received or anticipates a request for direct transmission to a foreign authority.
  • Classify the purpose of each transmission: financial-market supervision or another purpose, because FINMA says different conditions apply.
  • Identify the information, client confidentiality, personal data, recipients, legal basis and any FINMA notification or approval step under the current framework.
  • If the issue is operationally material, compare the draft circular with the IAM's existing cross-border disclosure procedure and decide whether to submit consultation comments.
  • Do not change the production procedure solely on the basis of the consultation draft; update it after the final circular and commencement information are published.
Evidence
Applicability note; inventory of relevant transmissions or a documented no-current-use conclusion; legal analysis for any live request; consultation decision or response; and a monitoring item for the final circular.
