Swiss sanctions · Binding Swiss measure

Switzerland adds 53 Russia-related sanctions targets

What changed: the EAER amended Annexes 2 and 8 of the Ukraine Ordinance on 10 August 2026. The official delta added nine individuals and 44 entities to Annex 8, amended six existing Annex 8 records and amended five Annex 2 end-user entries. The change took effect on 11 August 2026 at 23:00. [S2] [S3, pp. 1–23]

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Action required. The Annex 8 financial sanctions apply in Switzerland. They require the freezing of funds and economic resources owned or controlled by listed persons or entities, prohibit making funds or economic resources available to them directly or indirectly, and require reporting of frozen assets to SECO. [S1] [S4, Article 15]
Responsible person / function
Proposed internal allocation based on the IAM's current structure:
Primary execution: outsourced compliance/AML provider
Internal control and escalation: designated AML Officer. Portfolio and issuer exposure: in-house Risk Manager and Portfolio Management. Ownership/control and legal-nexus analysis: in-house Legal Counsel. Freezing, reporting and material-case oversight: Directors.
Due / trigger
Legally effective: 11 August 2026, 23:00. The restrictions are already in force. The Ordinance and FINMA notice state no later calendar deadline for completing a full-book re-screen; that internal completion date is therefore To be set internally. Proposed internal trigger: do not process an affected instruction before a potential match is resolved. [S1] [S2] [S4, Article 15]
Status
Open · effective
  • Update the control source. Confirm that the screening vendor or manual process uses the SECO consolidated list dated 11 August 2026 or a later official version. [S2] [S4]
  • Re-screen relevant exposure. As a company-specific control response, include clients, beneficial owners and controllers, authorised persons, payment and transaction parties, portfolio issuers and securities, banks, custodians and other counterparties where funds or economic resources could be owned, controlled or made available. This scope is an inference from Article 15 and depends on the IAM's actual relationships and assets. [S4, Article 15]
  • Resolve more than name matches. For a possible hit, use the identifiers in the delta and assess ownership and control before concluding that the restriction does or does not apply. The new records include Russian defence and industrial businesses, oil and shipping companies, UAE and Hong Kong entities and an individual with a Geneva address; these particulars may help prioritise the review but do not replace full-list screening. [S3, Annex 8 additions]
  • Apply the binding measures to confirmed hits. Freeze affected funds or economic resources, do not make them available directly or indirectly, and report affected frozen assets or business relationships to SECO as required. [S1] [S4, Article 15]
  • Run the AMLA analysis separately. FINMA states that a SECO report does not replace the additional clarification required by Article 6 AMLA; if suspicion cannot be dispelled, an immediate report to MROS under Article 9 AMLA may be required. [S1] [S7, Articles 6 and 9]
  • Check Annex 2 only where relevant. The delta amends five existing restricted end-user entries. This is principally a goods/export-control change and needs a separate assessment only if the IAM finances, holds or otherwise participates in activity affected by those end-user restrictions. [S3, Annex 2 amendments]
Evidence
The official publications prescribe the prohibitions and reporting duties but do not prescribe a special evidence format for this re-screen. The internal file can record the list version and screening date, population screened, results, match-resolution and ownership/control analysis, restrictions applied, and any SECO or MROS report or decision. This evidencing format is a proposed internal control response.

What the official delta establishes

  • Annex 8 additions: nine individuals and 44 entities were added under the financial-sanctions and entry/transit provisions. [S3, pp. 1–19]
  • Annex 8 amendments: five existing individual records and one existing entity record were changed. [S3, pp. 19–21]
  • Annex 2 amendments: the entries for UEC-Saturn, JSC AeroKompozit, Baikal Electronics, the 48th Central Scientific Research Institute and Tellur Elektroniks were amended. [S3, pp. 21–23]
  • Source of listings: the delta identifies the origin of these amendments as the European Union; they became binding in Switzerland through the Swiss ordinance update. [S3]

Effective-date clarification

FINMA published its notice on 12 August and describes the measures as entering into force “today” at 23:00. The SECO Ukraine page and the official delta both expressly state 11 August 2026 at 23:00. This brief uses the SECO date because the SECO publication and the operative delta agree; the practical conclusion is that the amendment is already in force. [S1] [S2] [S3, p. 1]
