UK FCDO · 12 Nov · Sanctions

UK lists Russian energy companies and specifies nine ships

What changed: The UK Russia notice adds 11 designations and nine ship specifications and varies one entry. The companies include SIBUR Holding, Slavneft, Vostok Oil, NIPIGAZ and SUEK. [S1]

Why it matters: High if the IAM has a matched issuer, counterparty, ownership or payment exposure and an applicable UK legal or provider-policy nexus; otherwise conditional.

What the notice actually adds

The official Russia-regime notice records 20 additions: 11 designations and nine specified ships, plus one variation. Named companies include JSC NIPIGAZPERERABOTKA, PJSC SIBUR HOLDING, PJSC Oil and Gas Company Slavneft, LLC Vostok Oil and JSC Siberian Coal Energy Company. Use the entry’s legal identifiers and applicable measures, not a headline about all Russian energy companies. [S1] [S2]

What a Swiss IAM should check

Where an issuer, client, beneficial owner, counterparty or service chain matches, establish the actual UK nexus or provider restriction. The designation may affect dealing with funds or making funds or resources available; a ship specification has a different set of restrictions. A Swiss-only relationship is not brought under UK law merely because Switzerland has also imposed Russia-related sanctions. [S1, sanctions imposed and guidance] [S3, regulations 3 and 11–15]
