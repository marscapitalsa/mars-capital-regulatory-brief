OFAC · 21 Oct · Potentially relevant

Lukoil International: OFAC extends limited sale and wind-down permissions

What changed: General License 131K replaced 131J. It permits specified contingent sale negotiations and necessary maintenance or wind-down for Lukoil International and its covered entities; it does not authorise completion of the sale or payments to Russia. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to a Swiss IAM. High only for affected Lukoil International exposure; low otherwise. This is a US authorisation, not an independent permission under Swiss law.

Negotiation does not mean completion

Paragraph (a) covers negotiation and entry into contingent contracts involving the sale, disposition or transfer of Lukoil International GmbH or entities it owns directly or indirectly at least 50%, individually or in aggregate. Performance must be expressly contingent on separate OFAC authorisation. [S1, para. (a)]

Operations and payments are treated separately

Paragraph (b) permits ordinarily incident and necessary maintenance or wind-down. Paragraph (c) permits use of covered blocked accounts for paragraph (b) transactions. Paragraph (d) preserves other restrictions, including transfers to a person or account in Russia and unblocking outside paragraph (c). [S1, paras. (b)–(d)]
