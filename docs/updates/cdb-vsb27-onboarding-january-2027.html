FINMA · 10 Nov · AML / Onboarding

CDB 27 recognition: coordinate bank onboarding changes

What changed: FINMA recognised the revised Swiss banks’ due-diligence agreement, CDB/VSB 27, as a minimum standard. It starts on 1 January 2027, with a four-month transition and no wholesale replacement of existing forms required. [S1]

Why it matters: Medium; higher if the IAM collects bank KYC documents under delegated onboarding arrangements. Bank requirements and the IAM’s own regulatory rules should not be assumed identical.

Bank standard and IAM application are different questions

FINMA’s recognition and the updated references in AMLO-FINMA concern the institutions within the relevant provisions. A portfolio manager should first identify its own applicable framework and any contractual delegation by a bank. The banking agreement’s title does not make its whole rulebook directly applicable to every independent manager. [S1] [S2, Art. 1] [S3, Arts. 35, 40 and 41]

Forms and digital processes

CDB 27 contains rules for identifying clients and controlling persons, establishing beneficial ownership and documenting those steps. Article 28(5) permits beneficial-owner declarations using a procedure under FINMA’s video and online identification provisions. This does not mean any unsigned PDF or unverified email is automatically acceptable: use the relevant bank’s compliant process and the applicable provision. [S2, Arts. 20–28 and 43–46]

Transition and existing files

Article 67 sets 1 January 2027 as commencement. Article 68 provides a four-month transition; the new provisions apply to relationships newly established after that period or when identification or beneficial-owner determination is repeated. Article 68(3) says existing forms need not be replaced for existing relationships. This does not dispense with other event-driven or risk-based updating duties. [S2, Arts. 67–68]
