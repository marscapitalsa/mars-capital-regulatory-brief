OFAC · 15 Oct · Potentially relevant

OFAC extends the Lukoil retail-station permission to April 2027

What changed: General License 128D replaces 128C and extends specified transactions for Lukoil International’s physical retail service stations outside Russia until 12:01 a.m. EDT on 24 April 2027. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. Medium conditional relevance. The 15 October FAQ expressly distinguishes the retail licence from the separate LIG negotiation/maintenance/wind-down licence; the longer retail date must not be applied to the broader exposure.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Relevant only to affected LIG entities and activities, with a US-law, secondary-sanctions or counterparty-policy issue. The retail permission does not generally authorise trading Lukoil securities.
Responsible person / function
Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material restrictions, exceptions and policy changes: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
GL 128D expires 24 April 2027 · the broader GL 131J still expires 22 October 2026.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • Identify whether the activity concerns physical retail stations outside Russia and an entity within the licence’s LIG definition. Check the activity against paragraph (a). [S1]
  • Check the exclusions, including other blocked persons, Directives 2 and 4 and transfers to persons or accounts in Russia. [S1, paragraph (c)]
  • If relying on a broader LIG wind-down or sale process, use the separate GL 131J terms and expiry rather than the GL 128D retail date. Obtain the bank or custodian’s confirmation of the applicable permission. [S2]
Evidence
Entity and activity analysis, exact licence version, payment destination and counterparty confirmation; Legal review of the permission used.
