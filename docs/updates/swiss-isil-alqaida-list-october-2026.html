SECO / FINMA · 15–16 Oct · Action required

Swiss ISIL/Al-Qaida records amended: refresh screening and resolve any match

What changed: SECO amended two individual records and one entity record, effective 15 October and published on 16 October. These are updates to existing listings, with financial sanctions continuing to apply. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. High relevance for list maintenance. Confirm the provider loaded the delta, resolve any match using the revised identifiers, and apply the SECO and AMLA escalation chain where warranted.

Application to the IAM

  • Confirm the screening dataset incorporates the 16 October delta; use the three UN identifiers to check that existing matched or dismissed records were reassessed where the identifying data changed. [S1]
  • For a confirmed affected relationship, implement the prohibitions, freeze the sanctioned assets and report the relationship to SECO as required by the ordinance. [S2]
  • Where suspicious indicators arise, perform the additional clarifications under Article 6 AMLA. If suspicion cannot be dispelled, report immediately to MROS under Article 9 AMLA; the SECO report does not replace this duty. [S2]
Evidence
Provider update confirmation, delta screening result and match-resolution record; where applicable, freezing decision, SECO report, AMLA clarifications and MROS filing.
