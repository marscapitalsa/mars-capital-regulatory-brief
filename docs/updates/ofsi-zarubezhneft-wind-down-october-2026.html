OFSI · 8 Oct · Potentially relevant

OFSI opens a time-limited Zarubezhneft wind-down route

What changed: General Licence INT/2026/11080040 permits wind-down or divestment from transactions involving Zarubezhneft JSC or entities it owns or controls, including closing positions and necessary supporting activity. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. High conditional relevance if Zarubezhneft exposure exists. The permission is limited to wind-down/divestment and does not generally authorise new business.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Relevant to an affected transaction with a UK nexus or provider reliance on the UK licence. The covered subsidiaries may be incorporated anywhere; ownership/control follows regulation 7.
Responsible person / function
Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material procedure changes, restrictions or exceptions: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
Expires 23:59 on 21 November 2026 · UK-law or relevant provider-policy nexus.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • Identify the affected entity, existing transaction and ownership/control position; match the proposed activity to clauses 3–4. [S1]
  • Ask the custodian, bank or broker whether it can execute under the licence, and determine any remaining prohibition before instruction. Clause 6 preserves restrictions not licensed. [S1]
  • If the permission is used, retain accurate, complete and readable records for at least six years. Recheck the licence at execution because OFSI may vary, suspend or revoke it. [S1, clauses 5, 9]
Evidence
Entity and nexus analysis; applicable licence version; provider response; wind-down approval and execution records, retained for the licence’s six-year minimum if used.
