EU · 12 Oct · Potentially relevant

EU lists 826 Russian companies and 743 executives

What changed: The EU added 1,569 names under its Ukraine territorial-integrity regime: 826 companies in Russia’s military-industrial complex and 743 executives. The measures include an asset freeze and a ban on making funds or economic resources available. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. High conditional relevance: the size of the addition makes a complete list refresh and client/UBO/issuer exposure review more useful than a search for a few prominent names.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Relevant where an EU person or transaction nexus exists, or a custodian, broker, bank, fund or contract applies EU lists. Swiss adoption of this October package was not identified by 18 October.

Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material restrictions, exceptions and policy changes: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
EU measures effective 12 October 2026 · internal review: To be set internally.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • Where EU lists apply, confirm the provider incorporated Regulation 2026/2281, not merely a sample of names from the announcement. [S1, S2]
  • Apply the new annex to clients, beneficial owners, issuers and counterparties; investigate a possible match against the official identifiers before determining restrictions.
  • Identify the EU or contractual nexus and request the relevant custodian’s position for affected securities, fund holdings or payments. Treat Swiss-law applicability separately.
Evidence
List-version confirmation; match and exposure results; identity and nexus analysis; custodian response and resulting restriction or no-exposure conclusion.
