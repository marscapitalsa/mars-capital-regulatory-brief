European Union · Sanctions · 28 September 2026

EU lists 10 people under its Russia human-rights regime

What changed: the Council listed 10 individuals for supporting repression of democratic opposition and undermining democracy and the rule of law in Russia. The publication identifies judges and prosecutors connected with barring Yabloko from the State Duma elections and with proceedings against opposition politicians. Listed persons are subject to an EU asset freeze and EU persons may not make funds or economic resources available to them. [S1]

Separate regime. These entries were made under the EU framework concerning the human-rights situation in Russia, not the EU territorial-integrity regime and not Swiss law. The distinction matters when resolving a match and determining the governing prohibition.
