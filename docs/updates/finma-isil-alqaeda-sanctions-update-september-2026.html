Switzerland · Sanctions · 29 September 2026

ISIL/Al-Qaeda list changed: screening and AML escalation remain separate duties

What changed: the responsible UN committee amended its list on 28 September. FINMA states that the change is directly applicable in Switzerland and that SECO updated the Swiss SESAM sanctions database on 29 September. Financial intermediaries are instructed to implement the prohibitions, freeze affected assets and report affected relationships to SECO. [S1]

Immediate control point. A SECO report does not replace the separate AMLA analysis. FINMA states that suspicious circumstances require additional clarification under Article 6 AMLA and, if suspicion cannot be dispelled, immediate reporting to MROS under Article 9 AMLA. [S1]

Status
List update to verify
  • Obtain evidence that the screening provider loaded the 29 September SESAM change and record the effective list version or timestamp.
  • Re-screen the configured client, beneficial-owner, controlling-person, signatory, counterparty and relevant portfolio population.

  • Separately perform Article 6 AMLA clarification and document the Article 9 MROS decision; a SECO filing does not discharge that duty.
