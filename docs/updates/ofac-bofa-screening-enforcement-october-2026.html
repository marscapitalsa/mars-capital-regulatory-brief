OFAC · 13 Oct · Potentially relevant

OFAC case exposes failures in screening updates, alert priority and payment routing

What changed: Bank of America agreed to pay $11,662,930 for 475 apparent sanctions violations. OFAC identified blocked-bank routing, low priority for exact name-and-birth-date matches, watchlist-update delays and account-handling errors. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. The case gives concrete controls to discuss with the outsourced AML provider and executing banks: successful list delivery is insufficient if activation, alert queues or suppression rules fail.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions Outsourcing
Medium relevance as enforcement lessons for an IAM using outsourced screening or US-linked payments. The settlement creates no new generally applicable Swiss rule.

Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material restrictions, exceptions and policy changes: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
Case-specific settlement · internal review: To be set internally.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • For any screening service used, review how updates are confirmed as active and how failed updates are escalated; OFAC identified both vendor and internal update failures. [S1, pages 2–3]
  • Review exact-name/date-of-birth priority, false-positive suppression and migration backlogs against the risks identified in the release. [S1, pages 2–3 and 5]
  • Where a relevant payment-routing or screening weakness is found, establish the affected period and population and assess a proportionate lookback. Apply existing legal and contractual escalation duties rather than treating the settlement as a new Swiss mandate. [S1, pages 1–2 and 5]
Evidence
Provider responses and existing control results; where a gap is found, affected-period assessment, remedial decision and completion evidence.
