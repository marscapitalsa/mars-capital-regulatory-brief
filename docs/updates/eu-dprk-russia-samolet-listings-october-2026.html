EU · 12 Oct · Potentially relevant

EU lists Samolet Group and other Russia–DPRK cooperation targets

What changed: The EU added three people and four entities under its autonomous DPRK regime, including Samolet Group and CEO Anna Akinshina, Black Horse and Vladimir Sokolov, Golden Autumn Agroholding, Paekyangsan Shipping and Ri Song Jin. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. High conditional relevance where portfolios contain Samolet or related issuers. A Russia-only sanctions filter could miss the entries because the legal regime is DPRK.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Relevant to EU-linked or contractually EU-screened clients, issuers, bonds, funds and payments. These are autonomous EU DPRK listings; they do not automatically become Swiss restrictions.
Responsible person / function
Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material restrictions, exceptions and policy changes: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
EU measures effective 12 October 2026 · internal review: To be set internally.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • Where EU lists are applied, include Regulation 2026/2278 in the update and check the new entries across regimes rather than filtering screening only to Russia. [S1, S2]
  • Review any client, issuer, bond, fund or counterparty exposure to Samolet Group and the other named targets, using the annex identifiers to establish the legal entity.
  • Determine the EU or contractual nexus, obtain the custodian’s treatment and assess owned-or-controlled entities under the applicable rules before instructing a trade or payment.
Evidence
Cross-regime screening results, issuer/entity identification, portfolio exposure and custodian response, nexus analysis and resulting decision.
