United Kingdom · Sanctions · 1–2 October 2026

UK adds 23 targets and eight ships, then remakes two ship specifications

What changed: the 1 October notice added 31 entries: 23 individuals and entities and eight ships. The people and entities are subject to the sanctions specified in their entries, including asset freezes, trust-services sanctions and director-disqualification sanctions; individuals also face travel bans. The ships are subject to shipping and trade sanctions. On 2 October, the UK revoked and remade the specifications for GALLE ENERGY and BEBEK-E under new entries and corrected two individual records. [S1] [S2] [S3]

Operational consequence. The 2 October notice did not release GALLE ENERGY or BEBEK-E. It revoked their 1 October entries and added them again under new UK identifiers. Screening or restriction evidence should therefore use the list state after the 2 October remake. [S3]

Conditional action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
UK measures apply where UK law supplies a nexus, including a relevant UK person, entity, branch, service or transaction chain. They may also affect the IAM where a custodian, broker, bank, fund, platform or contract applies the UK list.
Responsible person / function
Primary: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
UK and contractual nexus: in-house Legal Counsel. Client, portfolio and payment exposure: Portfolio Management and Operations. Challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material restriction or exit oversight: Directors.
Due / trigger
UK list changes took effect on 1 and 2 October 2026. For the Swiss IAM, action is triggered by a possible match and a UK or contractual nexus. Internal confirmation date: To be set internally.
Status
Nexus-dependent
  • Confirm that any provider applying UK sanctions loaded the 1 October additions and the 2 October remade ship specifications and corrections.
  • Screen clients, beneficial owners, controlling persons, authorised persons, issuers, counterparties, funds, payments and relevant shipping exposure against the current entries.
  • Pay particular attention to the designated energy entity Novatek Gas and Power Asia Pte Ltd and to any security, fund or payment exposure connected with it, while applying ownership and control rules rather than name matching alone.
  • For a possible match, identify the exact measure and UK or contractual nexus. The same package contains asset-freeze measures for persons and entities and shipping/trade measures for vessels.
  • Do not treat the 2 October revocations of the two original ship records as delistings: both ships were added again in the same notice.
Evidence
Provider-update confirmation showing the 2 October list state; screening results; identity, ownership and control analysis; ship-identifier review where relevant; UK or contractual nexus assessment; counterparty communication; and restriction or no-action decision.

Package composition

  • The UK press release states that the package comprises 23 individuals and entities and eight ships. It describes eight people connected with detention, torture or ill-treatment, seven connected with indoctrination or militarisation of children and seven connected with pro-Kremlin disinformation. [S1]
  • The vessel group comprises three shadow-fleet ships, two bunkering vessels and three Russian-owned ice-class vessels. [S1]
  • The 1 October notice identifies 31 additions. The 2 October notice identifies two additions, two corrections and two revocations; the additions and revocations concern the same two ships under remade entries. [S2] [S3]
