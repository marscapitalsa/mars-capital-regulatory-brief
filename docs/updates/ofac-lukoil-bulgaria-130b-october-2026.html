OFAC · 15 Oct · Potentially relevant

OFAC extends permission for four named Lukoil Bulgaria entities

What changed: General License 130B replaces 130A and permits specified EO 14024 transactions involving the named Bulgarian Lukoil entities and their majority-owned entities until 12:01 a.m. EDT on 24 April 2027. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. Medium conditional relevance for portfolios, funds or payments specifically connected to the four Bulgarian entities.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Relevant to an affected entity, asset or payment where US law or the executing provider’s OFAC policy matters. It is not a group-wide permission for all Lukoil affiliates.
Responsible person / function
Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material restrictions, exceptions and policy changes: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
GL 130B expires 12:01 a.m. EDT on 24 April 2027 · internal review: To be set internally.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • Check whether the entity is Lukoil Neftohim Burgas JSC, Lukoil Bulgaria EOOD, Lukoil Aviation Bulgaria EOOD or Lukoil Bulgaria Bunker EOOD, or is at least 50% owned by them directly or indirectly, individually or in aggregate. [S1, paragraph (a)]
  • Apply paragraph (b): other prohibited transactions and other blocked Lukoil affiliates remain outside this permission unless separately authorised. [S1]
  • Ask the custodian or payment bank which licence supports the proposed activity and keep its exact entity and transaction scope with the decision.
Evidence
Entity and ownership mapping, proposed transaction, licence copy and bank/custodian response.
