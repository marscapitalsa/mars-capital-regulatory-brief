Switzerland · 4 Nov · FINMA reminder 6 Nov · Action required

Switzerland adds 254 asset-freeze listings

What changed: Switzerland added 254 individuals and entities to asset-freeze and non-availability measures and removed six individuals and one company. FINMA reminded financial intermediaries that reporting to SECO does not replace the separate AMLA assessment. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to a Swiss IAM. High. Applies to Swiss relationships and assets that match the updated Annex 8 or fall within Article 15 through ownership, control or acting on behalf or instructions.

A freeze is different from the listed-bank transaction ban

Article 15(1) freezes assets and economic resources owned or controlled by the specified persons or entities, including certain agents and owned or controlled entities. Article 15(2) separately prohibits providing assets or making assets or economic resources available to them, directly or indirectly. Check the Swiss entry and the relationship rather than assuming that an EU listing or a transaction-ban entry is an Annex 8 freeze. [S1] [S2] [S3]

Reporting can cover pre-listing activity

Article 16(1) requires prompt SECO reporting where a person holds or manages assets, or knows of assets or resources, that must be assumed to be subject to the freeze. Article 16(1bis) separately requires prompt reporting of transactions in the two weeks before the relevant Annex 8 listing where its conditions are met. The annual balance filing is for financial institutions that have reported assets they hold or manage; it is not a general nil return. [S3]

Separate Swiss AMLA assessment

FINMA says a SECO report does not discharge additional clarification under Article 6 AMLA where suspicion arises. If the relevant suspicion cannot be dispelled, assess and, where Article 9 applies, report to MROS without delay. [S4] [S5]

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions AML
Refresh Swiss screening. For a confirmed exposure, assess the freeze, SECO reporting and the two-week transaction lookback; assess AMLA separately.
Responsible person / function
Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider for screening. In-house Legal Counsel: legal scope, ownership and reporting analysis. In-house Risk Manager: review of affected exposures. Directors: oversight of material cases under internal delegations.
Due / trigger
The listings took effect on 4 November 2026 at 23:00 Swiss time. Report without delay where Article 16(1) or (1bis) applies. Any annual return under Article 16(1quater) is due by 15 February for holdings as at 31 December.
Status
Review required

Application to the IAM

  1. Load the official Swiss delta and check clients, beneficial owners, counterparties and relevant held assets for affected relationships. Review delistings against any separate applicable restriction before changing a control. [S2] [S3]
  2. For an affected relationship, distinguish what the IAM manages or knows from what the custodian holds; coordinate execution of the freeze, and assess and fulfil the IAM’s own reporting duty rather than presuming the custodian’s report discharges it. Check the preceding two weeks under Article 16(1bis). [S3] [S4]
  3. Document the AMLA assessment separately; where suspicion triggers Articles 6 or 9, follow the corresponding clarification and reporting procedure. [S4] [S5]
Evidence
Swiss list match and ownership/control assessment; record of any applicable restriction; transaction review, SECO report and AMLA assessment where triggered.
