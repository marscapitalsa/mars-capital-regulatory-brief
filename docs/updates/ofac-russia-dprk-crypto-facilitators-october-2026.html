US Treasury · 28 Oct · Potentially relevant

OFAC lists Russia-linked facilitators of North Korean IT-worker fraud

What changed: OFAC listed individuals involved in Russia-based networks that supported North Korean IT workers. The entries include digital-asset identifiers; Treasury described a Russian facilitator’s role in converting and transferring cryptocurrency. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to a Swiss IAM. Conditional. The US listings are under the DPRK programme, not a blanket restriction on Russian IT providers or crypto wallets. Their relevance depends on the exact match and applicable legal or contractual scope.

What Treasury identified

Treasury described Russia-based delegations supporting North Korean IT work and the use of crypto to transfer proceeds. It said Ilya Aleksandrovich Boykov helped Alis exchange and convert the equivalent of $3.5 million in crypto transactions. OFAC’s entries include ETH and TRX identifiers for Boykov and an ETH identifier for another listed individual. [S1] [S2]

When it matters to the IAM

If a service provider, payment beneficiary, wallet or beneficial owner matches the new entries, assess the actual party and the applicable US legal or provider-policy nexus. Where no match or relevant exposure is identified, the notice does not by itself establish a new Swiss restriction on the IAM’s vendors. [S1, Sanctions Implications] [S2]
