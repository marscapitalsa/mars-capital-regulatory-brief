EU · 8 Oct · Potentially relevant

EU extends its Russia hybrid-activities listings to 9 October 2027

What changed: The Council prolonged the existing regime by one year. It continues to cover 80 individuals and 20 entities with asset freezes and non-availability restrictions; the announcement adds no new names. [S1]

Relevance to an IAM. Low conditional relevance for maintaining existing restrictions. Check continuity if a provider or internal rule has an expiry attached to the regime.

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Relevant where EU law or a counterparty’s EU-list policy applies. This EU extension does not automatically become Swiss law.

Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Portfolio exposure, where relevant: Portfolio Management. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material procedure changes, restrictions or exceptions: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
Regime extended until 9 October 2027 · internal review: To be set internally.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • If EU lists apply, confirm that the existing entries remained active after the former end date. [S1]
  • Resolve any existing match under the applicable EU or contractual rules; the extension alone does not create a new match.
  • If no relevant exposure or EU-list use exists, retain the item for monitoring.
Evidence
Where applicable, provider confirmation and the existing exposure/nexus assessment.
