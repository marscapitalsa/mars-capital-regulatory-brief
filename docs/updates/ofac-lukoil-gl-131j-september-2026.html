United States · OFAC · 18 September 2026

OFAC extends the Lukoil International negotiation and wind-down licence

What changed: General License 131J extends specified authorisations concerning Lukoil International GmbH and entities it owns 50% or more. It covers negotiations, due diligence and contingent contracts for a possible sale, plus specified maintenance and wind-down activity, until 12:01 a.m. EDT on 22 October 2026. [S1] [S2]

Important limitation. The licence does not authorise completion of a sale, transfer or disposition and does not generally unblock property. A contingent contract must remain expressly conditional on separate OFAC authorisation. [S2]

Status
Exposure-dependent
  • Identify direct and indirect exposure to Lukoil International GmbH and entities owned 50% or more.
  • Obtain the custodian or broker's written position on what activity it will process under GL 131J.
  • Separate maintenance or wind-down, negotiation and due-diligence activity from the actual completion of a sale or any unblocking.

  • Do not rely on GL 131J for a transaction after the expiry time unless OFAC issues a further licence.

Exposure search; ownership analysis; US-nexus assessment; custodian or broker response; licence-condition analysis; transaction documents; and expiry tracking where the licence is relied upon.
