UK · 8–15 Oct · Potentially relevant

UK adds 38 Russia-related targets and updates an existing individual listing

What changed: On 8 October, the UK added 26 persons/entities and specified 12 ships, varied two entries and later corrected three records. On 15 October it varied Nadia Cherkasova’s existing entry; her asset freeze and other listed restrictions continue. [S1] [S2] [S3] [S4] [S5]

Relevance to an IAM. High conditional relevance for Russian-client payment chains, crypto services and energy exposure. The package includes Zarubezhneft, INK Capital and finance/crypto intermediaries.
