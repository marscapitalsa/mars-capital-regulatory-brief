Swiss sanctions · Binding Swiss list amendment

SECO updates 610 restricted-end-user records and delists Yelo Bank

What changed: the EAER amendment dated 14 August updated four individual and 606 entity records in Annex 2 and removed Yelo Bank (Azerbaijan) from Annex 14a. It took effect on 17 August 2026 at 23:00. The delta contains no new Annex 8 targets and introduces no new category of prohibited activity. [S1] [S2, pp. 1–127] [S3]

Action plan

Applicability
Sanctions
Conditional. The Annex 2 changes matter if the IAM participates in financing, financial assistance or another activity caught by Articles 4–6 or 35(4) in relation to a listed end-user. The Yelo Bank delisting matters if a client, payment, counterparty or service chain uses that bank. This amendment is not an Annex 8 asset-freeze update. [S2] [S5, Articles 4–6, 27a and 35(4)]
Responsible person / function
Proposed internal allocation based on the IAM's current structure:
Primary exposure check: outsourced compliance/AML provider
Internal control and escalation: designated AML Officer. Portfolio and payment-chain exposure: in-house Risk Manager and Portfolio Management. Legal scope, other-regime and contractual analysis: in-house Legal Counsel. Any material relaxation of an existing restriction: Directors.
Due / trigger
Legally effective: 17 August 2026, 23:00. The official amendment sets no separate completion deadline for a Swiss IAM. Internal verification date: To be set internally. [S1] [S2, p. 1]
Status
Conditional review
  • Confirm the operative list version. Check that the screening vendor or manual control uses the SECO consolidated list dated 17 August 2026 or a later official version. [S1] [S4]
  • Do not classify the Annex 2 records as new asset-freeze targets. All 610 records are marked “amended”, not “listed”. Annex 2 identifies end-users for the existing goods and export-control restrictions referenced in the delta. [S2, pp. 1–127] [S3, sanctions-set 29445]
  • Check Annex 2 only where the activity creates the relevant nexus. If the IAM finances or otherwise supports a transaction involving controlled goods, technology or a listed end-user, use the amended record and the applicable article to assess the transaction. The amendment alone does not establish a blanket prohibition on holding every security issued by an Annex 2 entity; that conclusion depends on the activity addressed by Articles 4–6 or 35(4). [S2] [S5, Articles 4–6 and 35(4)]
  • Reassess Yelo Bank only if there is exposure. Article 27a(2) prohibits direct or indirect transactions with Annex 14a entities. Yelo Bank's removal means that this specific Annex 14a basis no longer identifies the bank after the effective time. Before changing an existing block or control, check the current Swiss consolidated list, any other applicable sanctions regime, and contractual requirements imposed by the bank or custodian. [S2, p. 127] [S4] [S5, Article 27a]
  • No Annex 8 re-screen is triggered by new names in this delta. The XML contains 611 changed targets only: 610 amendments under Annex 2 and the Yelo Bank delisting under Annex 14a. No target in the delta is assigned to the Annex 8 financial-sanctions set. [S3]
Evidence
The Ordinance does not prescribe a special file format for this list-maintenance event. Where the update is relevant, the internal record can retain the list version used, the affected relationship or transaction, the applicable annex and article, the conclusion reached, and the approval for any change to an existing restriction. This is a proposed internal control response.

What the official delta establishes

  • Annex 2: four individual and 606 entity records were amended. The PDF republishes names, aliases, addresses and other available identifiers, but does not provide a field-by-field redline. [S2, pp. 1–127] [S3]
  • Annex 14a: Yelo Bank (Azerbaijan), SSID 175-103912, was delisted from the entities linked to Article 27a. [S2, p. 127] [S3, sanctions-set 78348]
  • No additions: the amendment contains no record marked as newly listed and no Annex 8 financial-sanctions entry. It therefore does not add an asset-freeze target. [S2] [S3]
  • No new activity category: the amendment changes annex records under the existing Articles 4–6, 27a and 35(4); it does not amend the operative wording of those provisions. [S2] [S5]

Practical conclusion for the IAM

This is a control-data and delisting update rather than a new Swiss asset-freeze package. It warrants a separate record because the amended Annex 2 identifiers may affect a relevant export-control assessment and the Yelo Bank delisting may change a payment or counterparty conclusion. If neither nexus exists and the screening service has already loaded the current SECO list, no further company-specific implementation step is apparent from this delta alone.
