European Union · Sanctions · 28 September 2026

EU lists 10 people and 17 entities over deportation of Ukrainian children

What changed: the Council added 10 individuals and 17 entities to the EU territorial-integrity sanctions regime for actions connected with unlawful deportation, forced transfer and assimilation of Ukrainian children. The listed persons and entities are subject to an asset freeze and EU persons are prohibited from making funds or economic resources available to them; the listed individuals also face travel bans. [S1]

Swiss-law distinction. The decision is an EU measure, not an automatic amendment of Swiss sanctions law. It affects the IAM where EU law supplies a nexus or a bank, custodian, broker, fund, platform or contract applies EU lists.

Who and what the measure covers

The Council states that the entities include the Moscow City Tourism Committee, Russian companies in children's recreation and tourism, children's camps and sports centres in Russia and occupied Ukrainian territories, and Songdowon International Children's Camp in the DPRK. Individuals include officials and managers connected with deportation, camps or indoctrination. These descriptions explain the policy basis; screening should still use the names and identifiers in the legal acts linked from the Council publication. [S1]
