FINMA · 8 Oct · Action required

FINMA changes digital client identification from 1 November

What changed: FINMA revised Circular 2016/7: Swiss e-ID and QR-coded official documents may be used, specified online routes require liveness detection, and equivalent digital processes may verify residence. The revised supervisory practice takes effect on 1 November. [S1] [S2]

Relevance to an IAM. High relevance if digital onboarding is used. Confirm the applicable method and provider readiness before the revised practice takes effect.

Action plan

Applicability
AML Onboarding Outsourcing
Applies to portfolio managers using video or online identification, including a delegated process. Check which route the IAM actually uses; conventional identification does not require adopting a digital route.
Responsible person / function
Execution: designated AML Officer and outsourced compliance/AML provider
Legal and nexus review: in-house Legal Counsel. Technical implementation, where required: onboarding/ICT provider. Control and challenge: in-house Risk Manager. Material procedure changes, restrictions or exceptions: Directors’ approval or oversight under the IAM’s organisational rules.
Due / trigger
Effective 1 November 2026 · margin 54 provides a one-year technical transition for the qualified-signature route only.
Status
Applicability to be confirmed

Application to the IAM

  • Identify each used method and compare the process with the corresponding circular section: video, electronic identity-document copy, qualified electronic signature, digital authentication or Swiss e-ID. [S1, S2]
  • For online routes, check liveness, document authenticity and residence verification under the applicable margins. E-ID requires validity and holder verification, residence verification and documentation. [S2, margins 31–44.4]
  • For third-party identification, verify professional knowledge and technology for the relevant countries; obtain and file the generated identification records. [S2, margin 51]
  • Apply the one-year transition only to the necessary residence-verification and liveness technical adjustments under margin 39; other changes take effect on 1 November. [S2, margin 54]
Evidence
For the used route: provider assessment, updated procedure where needed and retained identification records. The IAM’s Legal/Risk review and approval evidence depend on its existing governance arrangements.
